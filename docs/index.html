Privacy Policy — ETA Alert

Last updated: 3 September 2026

ETA Alert is an Android app that watches live traffic and sends you one notification when the drive time to your destination drops below a threshold you choose. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your data.

In short: ETA Alert has no servers and no accounts. Your location and your destination go directly from your phone to Google Maps to calculate a drive time. The developer never receives, stores, or sees any of your data.

What the app collects

  • Approximate and precise location. Collected while you are actively tracking a trip, including when the app is in the background or the screen is off. It is the starting point for the drive-time calculation.
  • Your destination. The address or place name you type in.
  • Your Google Maps API key. You create this yourself in Google Cloud Console and paste it into the app.
  • Your settings. Alert threshold and tracking duration.

Why location is used in the background

The whole purpose of the app is to keep checking traffic while you get on with something else. To do that it runs a foreground service that requests your current location roughly every three minutes and asks Google for the current drive time. A persistent notification is shown the entire time this is happening, so you always know when the app is active.

Tracking stops automatically as soon as one of these happens: the drive time drops below your threshold and you are alerted, the tracking window you set expires, or you press Stop.

Where your data goes

Your location and destination are sent from your device directly to Google's Maps Platform services (Routes API, Places API, and Geocoding API), using the API key you supplied. Those requests are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google Maps Platform Terms of Service.

ETA Alert sends data to no other destination. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting service, and no developer-operated backend of any kind.

What is stored, and where

Everything the app remembers — your API key, destination, threshold, duration, and tracking state — is stored in Android's private SharedPreferences storage on your device. That area is readable only by ETA Alert. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Your location is never written to storage. It is held in memory only long enough to make a single request, then discarded.

Data sharing and selling

No data is sold. No data is shared with third parties other than the direct Google Maps requests described above, which are required for the app to function.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling ETA Alert removes everything the app has stored. You can also clear it at any time via Android Settings → Apps → ETA Alert → Storage → Clear data. Because nothing is held on any server, there is no deletion request to submit.

Permissions and why each is needed

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — determine where the drive starts from.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION — keep checking traffic while the app is not on screen.
  • INTERNET — reach Google Maps.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show the tracking notification and the final alert.
  • WAKE_LOCK — stop the device sleeping through a scheduled traffic check.

Children

ETA Alert is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
